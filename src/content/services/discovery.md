Most projects go wrong before the first commit. Someone described the idea in a
meeting, everyone nodded, and six weeks later two people are describing different
products.

Discovery fixes that cheaply. We go through the requirements, I map what exists
today, and you get a document precise enough to build from — whether you build it
with me or with someone else.
