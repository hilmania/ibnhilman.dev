A fixed-scope build with weekly demos. You see working software every week, which
means you can redirect early instead of discovering a mismatch at the end.

### How the weeks run

Week 1 is setup and the core data model. Weeks 2–5 are features, one demo each
week. Week 6 is UAT with your users. Week 7 is go-live and handover.
